## 1. Description of Service

EinkNote is a lightweight text-writing application designed specifically for E-ink devices (optimized for Android 9 through Android 11, including Onyx POKE2 to POKE6 models). 

The App is built to eliminate distractions and minimize screen flicker:
* **"Just Write" Interface:** A clean, distraction-free writing environment resembling black ink on white paper.
* **Save Button:** Saves your note locally to the on-device local database.
* **TXT Button:** Exports your note as a standard plain text (`.txt`) file to your local device storage.
* **Google Button:** Connects via Google OAuth to export and convert your notes directly into a Google Docs file inside your personal Google Drive.
