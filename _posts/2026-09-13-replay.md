# EinkNote
**"Focus purely on writing on a crisp, paper-like E-ink screen—just like black ink on white paper."**
A lightweight text-writing app optimized for E-ink devices.
- Local text storage
- Export notes to Google Drive
- No sign-up or login required
- Minimized screen refreshes optimized for E-ink displays
